# Input/Output Setup Section

The **Input/Output Setup** menu configures how show data gets in and out of this
FPP device: incoming **Channel Inputs** (bridging E1.31/ArtNet/DDP/DMX from
another controller), outgoing **Channel Outputs** to your lights, any **Output
Processors** applied along the way, **Pixel Overlay Models** for virtual displays
and effects, and **GPIO Inputs** for physical buttons and sensors.
